# Nosok v36 — Payment / Document Intelligence / Assistant Bridges

## Payment

Use `billing_system` as the owner of payment and reconciliation. Nosok only requests charges and reads safe statuses through RPC contracts.

## Document Intelligence

Advisory-only document quality and OCR. No automated final approval/rejection.

## Assistant

Public assistant: general guidance only.  
Internal assistant: RBAC-scoped operational guidance.  
All policy-sensitive answers must be grounded in ministry policy snapshots.
